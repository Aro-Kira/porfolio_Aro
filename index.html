Pasan

Pasan

Overview
Pasan is a story about a young man who struggles to support his family despite their poverty and personal problems. When his efforts seem meaningless, he nearly gives up on life until his younger sister gives him a reason to keep going. Through a singing competition, he finds hope in pursuing his dream and changing their lives.

A young man carries the burden of supporting his family, with a mother addicted to gambling, a brother struggling with drug addiction, and a younger sister who depends on him. He works tirelessly selling goods to earn money for their daily needs, but his brother takes his hard-earned money, leaving him helpless and unable to pay their bills. Overwhelmed by his struggles, he reaches his breaking point and considers ending his life by jumping from a bridge. However, his younger sister finds him and begs him not to jump, reminding him that he still has a reason to live. Days later, he discovers an advertisement for a singing competition. Remembering his passion for singing, he decides to join, hoping to pursue his forgotten dream and find a way to lift his family out of poverty.
